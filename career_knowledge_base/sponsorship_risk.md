# Sponsorship risk

Phrases like no sponsorship, no visa sponsorship, US citizen required, green card required, permanent resident required, or must already be authorized to work in the United States can be important application risks for candidates who need visa support.

If a job says candidates must be authorized to work in the US but does not clearly say no sponsorship, the risk is usually medium rather than automatically high.

If a job explicitly mentions OPT, CPT, visa sponsorship, work visa support, or immigration support, the risk is lower and the candidate can prioritize fit based on skills and experience.
