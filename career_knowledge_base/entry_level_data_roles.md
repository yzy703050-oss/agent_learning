# Entry-level data roles

0-2 years, junior, new grad, and entry-level data roles are usually reasonable targets for candidates who can show projects, internships, coursework, or portfolio work.

For early-career candidates, a strong application should connect job requirements to concrete evidence: dashboards, SQL analysis, Python notebooks, experiment analysis, reporting workflows, or stakeholder communication examples.

If the job asks for 3+ years but the skill list is familiar and the title is not senior, it can still be worth applying. If the role says senior, lead, staff, or 5+ years, it is usually a weaker entry-level fit.
